### Hi, I'm Marcela Gotta. Mars, if we've met.

I build software that thinks with you.

I'm a senior software engineer in Madrid and Head Frontend Developer at Sngular, where I also co-lead the frontend chapter. Twelve years of product frontend, component systems and accessibility. Since 2024, also the infrastructure that lets AI agents work alongside people instead of living in a demo. I teach NLP and AI ethics in a master's programme, too.

**Building in the open**

- **Kimen** · Web Components for interfaces that agents generate at runtime. A guarded catalog, A2UI and MCP Apps adapters, and accessibility as a hard gate in CI. *Pre-1.0.*
- **Jezero** · A shared, searchable library of your agents' past work. Sessions go in, summaries and atoms come out, served read-only over MCP. *Pre-alpha.*
- **Orbit** · A macOS companion for the agent of your choice. It records and transcribes your meetings, writes the notes and lets you talk to your assistant from your Mac. *Beta.*

One thread runs through all three: what an agent shows you, what it remembers, and where it lives with you.

AI is the most powerful tool we've had, as long as people stay on the side that steers it.
